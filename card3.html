Card III / The digital self

The Web Weaver

My connections—both online and in real life—are integral to who I am. The Web Weaver reflects how I build networks, whether through social media, professional spaces, or friendships. It represents how my relationships are interconnected and how my data is shaped by the people I interact with. Everything I do online is part of a bigger web of interactions, and this card captures that.

Upright ↓ Reversed ↓

In balance

Upright meaning

  • Connection

    You are skillfully weaving relationships and networks, creating strong connections that support your growth. Collaboration is key, and you are finding ways to bring people together.

  • Influence

    You are at the center of a web of relationships or ideas, and your influence is growing. Trust your ability to connect people and ideas.

  • Collaboration

    The power of community is behind you, and by working with others, you are creating something greater than the sum of its parts.

In tension

Reversed meaning

  • Entanglement

    You may be caught up in a web of complicated relationships or obligations, leading to confusion or feeling trapped.

  • Isolation

    Despite being surrounded by people or networks, you feel disconnected. There is a lack of authentic connection, or you are trying to manage too many superficial ties.

  • Manipulation

    Be wary of manipulation—either from others or yourself—within your social or professional networks.

A prompt for reflection, shaped by my own digital life. Take a moment to consider what this card brings up for you.
